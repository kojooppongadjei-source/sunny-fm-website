The family of beloved gospel musician **Bernice Offei** has announced the arrangements for her funeral. All services will be held at the **Legon Interdenominational Church (LIC)** in November 2026.

## Remembrance night: Wednesday, November 11

A remembrance night will be held at the church on **Wednesday, November 11, at 7:00 pm**, giving family, friends and sympathisers the opportunity to honour her memory.

## Funeral service: Saturday, November 14

- **7:00 am:** Filing past
- **9:00 am – 11:00 am:** Burial service

Burial will be private. The final funeral rites will take place at the church after the service. The prescribed attire for Saturday is **black**.

## Thanksgiving service: Sunday, November 15

A thanksgiving service will follow on **Sunday, November 15, at 10:30 am**, also at LIC. Attendees are requested to wear **black and white**.

Sunny 88.7 FM extends its deepest condolences to the family, friends and all who were blessed by Bernice Offei's ministry in music. May she rest in the perfect peace of the Lord.
