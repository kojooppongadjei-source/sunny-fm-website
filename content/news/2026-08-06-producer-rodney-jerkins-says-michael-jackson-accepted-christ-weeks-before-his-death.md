Grammy-winning producer Rodney "Darkchild" Jerkins, who worked closely with Michael Jackson on several projects and built a personal friendship with him, has shared a striking detail about the singer's final weeks. Speaking in a recent interview on The Breakfast Club, Jerkins said he learned at Jackson's funeral that the entertainer had turned to Christ shortly before his death in 2009.

Jerkins said the news came from gospel singer Sandra Crouch and vocal coach Seth Riggs, who told him at the funeral, "Michael received Christ two weeks ago." For Jerkins, hearing that became, in his words, the most meaningful takeaway from Jackson's passing.

Jackson, raised in the Jehovah's Witness faith, was known throughout his career for referencing God and spirituality in interviews, fueling years of public curiosity about his beliefs. Jerkins' account offers fans a new detail in that long-running conversation about Jackson's faith journey.

The story has since circulated widely in Christian and gospel circles online, with many describing it as an encouraging reminder that a change of heart is possible at any point in a person's life, regardless of their past.
