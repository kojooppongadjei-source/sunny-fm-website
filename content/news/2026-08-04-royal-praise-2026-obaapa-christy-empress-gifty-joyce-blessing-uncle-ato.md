### About Obaapa Christy

Obaapa Christy is a famous Ghanaian gospel singer, praise and worship leader, and songwriter. Born Christiana Adwoa Twene on December 7 in New Suame, Kumasi, she is widely celebrated for her powerful vocals, deep voice, and numerous hit tracks.

**Early Life and Music Beginnings**

* Born to Mr. Akwesi Twene and Mary Amoah Twene as the second born of nine siblings.
* Began singing at a very young age, performing with the Kumasi-based group "The Hallelujah Voices."
* Discovered by veteran gospel musician Osuani Afrifa, who helped launch her professional music journey.
* Released her debut hit album, *Me Te Ase*, in 2002.
