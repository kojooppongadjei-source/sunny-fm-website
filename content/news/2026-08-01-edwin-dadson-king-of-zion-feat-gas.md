Edwin Dadson is back with a powerful declaration of faith. "King of Zion" is a soul-stirring anthem that lifts the name of Jesus as the ultimate King and ruler over all. Featuring the dynamic vocals of GAS, the track brings a sound that is both fresh and deeply spiritual.

Edwin Dadson has built a reputation in Ghana's gospel scene for anthems that pair contemporary production with unapologetically worship-centred lyrics, and "King of Zion" continues that pattern — built around a rising, congregational-style chorus designed to be sung as much as listened to. His collaborator on the track, Gifty Sekyi (GAS), brings a soulful vocal layer that gives the song its emotional lift in the second half, turning a straightforward declaration of faith into something closer to a live worship moment on record.

Lyrically, the song leans on the biblical image of Zion as God's dwelling place, using it to frame Jesus not just as Saviour but as reigning King over every circumstance — a theme that resonates with Sunny FM's daily programming around faith and encouragement.
