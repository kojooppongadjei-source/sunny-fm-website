Kino International Hotel has joined this year's Sunny FM Couples Retreat as a partner, describing the collaboration as a natural fit between two brands committed to family and community values.

Anita, a representative of the Tema-based hotel, told Sunny FM that the partnership goes beyond sponsorship and reflects a shared commitment to strengthening relationships and creating memorable experiences for couples and families.

"Sunny FM is also family-oriented and community-focused, and that's something that Kino also stands for," she said.

"It's not just sponsorship, but it's more about shared values and supporting the vision."

### About Kino International Hotel

Located in Tema Community 12, Kino International Hotel is approaching its first anniversary and has already welcomed guests from Ghana and abroad.

According to Anita, the hotel combines elements of Chinese culture with Ghanaian hospitality to create what it describes as more than simply a hotel stay.

"You're not just there for a stay. It's a whole experience," she said.

The hotel has 100 smart rooms, two restaurants offering Chinese, Ghanaian, continental and other international cuisine, as well as private dining spaces for couples, families and intimate gatherings.

One of its major attractions is what the hotel describes as the biggest swimming pool in Tema, which is available to individuals, groups and for swimming competitions.

Kino also has a gym with a dedicated instructor, a children's centre, conference and event facilities and extensive parking, including underground parking.

The hotel hosts weddings, corporate events and other social gatherings.

### A Destination for Families

For families looking for somewhere to spend time during the school vacation, Anita said Kino has increasingly become a destination for parents and children, particularly through its swimming facilities, children's centre and accommodation.

The hotel is also currently promoting a wellness package aimed at helping guests take a break from their normal routines and recharge.

The package combines accommodation and complimentary breakfast with stress-management coaching and access to the hotel's swimming pool and gym.

"Just pause for a moment and get rejuvenated," Anita said.

Kino also offers honeymoon and romantic packages for couples.

### Win a Weekend Getaway

As part of its partnership with the Sunny FM Couples Retreat, one participating couple will have the opportunity to win a weekend getaway at Kino International Hotel.

The partnership forms part of Sunny FM's activities celebrating marriage and relationships, with this year's Couples Retreat bringing couples together for time away from their regular routines to reconnect and strengthen their relationships.

Kino International Hotel is located in Tema Community 12, close to the Tema General Hospital and just off the motorway.

The hotel says more information about its accommodation, dining and hospitality packages is available through its official digital platforms.
