The strongest kingdom marriages are built on a shared assignment.

**Pastor Elvis Agyemang** and **Lady Mercy Agyemang** remind us that marriage is not only about building a home together, but also about advancing God's Kingdom together.

As they approach eight years of marriage, their journey continues to reflect what it means to pursue one vision with one heart. While Pastor Elvis Agyemang has become a global voice through **Grace Mountain Ministry** and the **Alpha Hour** prayer movement, Lady Mercy Agyemang has faithfully embraced the same assignment, serving with excellence through evangelism, Christian counseling, discipleship, and her ministry on Alpha Hour. Together, they have demonstrated that kingdom impact becomes even greater when purpose is shared.

Beyond the pulpit, their partnership is evident in every aspect of ministry. From hosting **Love Clinic** to strengthen marriages and relationships, to leading life-changing outreach initiatives and acts of compassion through Grace Mountain Ministry, they continue to show that the Gospel is not only preached but also lived through service, generosity, and love.

Together with their children, they are building more than a family. They are building a legacy of faith that is reaching lives across Ghana and around the world.

Their story reminds us that when God joins two people together, He doesn't only establish a marriage. He also entrusts them with an assignment that can influence generations.

Today, we celebrate Pastor Elvis Agyemang and Lady Mercy Agyemang for their unwavering commitment to Christ, to each other, and to the Kingdom. May the Lord continue to strengthen their union, enlarge their influence, and use their lives to inspire many more couples to pursue God's purpose together.
