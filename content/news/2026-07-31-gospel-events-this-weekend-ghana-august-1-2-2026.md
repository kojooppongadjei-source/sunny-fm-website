If you are looking for somewhere to worship, watch, or simply be encouraged this weekend, Ghana's gospel calendar has plenty on offer. Here is a roundup of what is happening on Saturday, August 1, and Sunday, August 2, 2026 — from concerts and special praise services to a cross-border worship night just over in Togo.

### Concerts and Special Praise Services

Harmonious Chorale Ghana is set to release a live musical collaboration this Sunday featuring gospel artiste Kofi Owusu Peprah. Fans are being encouraged to watch the group's official pages and local church broadcasts for the debut.

Over in Weija, Accra, the RCCG Tabernacle of Praise at White Cross Junction hosts its Cultural Sundays of Praise on Sunday morning from 9:00am to 12:00pm. The service leans into traditional and indigenous regional worship styles, offering something a little different from the typical Sunday format.

Gospel favourite Joe Mettle takes the spotlight this Sunday on Joy Prime TV's Evibes with Becky, where he is expected to minister live on the show.

### Just Across the Border: Sonnie Badu in Togo

For those near the eastern border, or simply up for a short trip, gospel artiste Dr Sonnie Badu headlines Glory Night, a stadium worship event at Stade de Kégué in Lomé, Togo, running both Saturday and Sunday evening. It is being billed as one of the bigger cross-border worship gatherings of the season.

### Keeping Up With Last-Minute Additions

Church programmes and campus gospel events have a habit of updating their schedules on short notice, so if you are planning to attend something specific, it is worth double-checking venue and time details directly with the organisers closer to the day.

Wherever you find yourself this weekend, we hope you carve out time to worship, be still, and be encouraged.
