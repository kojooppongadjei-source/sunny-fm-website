Lomé, Togo was filled with fireworks as worship, praise, and heartfelt devotion from thousands gathered erupted during Glory Night Lomé 2026.

Renowned Ghanaian gospel minister **Sonnie Badu** led thousands of worshippers in an unforgettable night of praise and worship during Glory Night at the **Stade de Kégué** in Lomé, Togo.

The highly anticipated worship gathering drew believers from across Togo and neighboring countries, creating an atmosphere filled with heartfelt praise, passionate worship, and a shared desire to exalt the name of Jesus Christ.

![](/assets/uploads/sonnie-badu-glory-night-stadium-crowd.jpg)

Throughout the evening, worshippers lifted their voices in songs of adoration and thanksgiving, with many describing the event as a life changing spiritual encounter marked by unity, hope, and the tangible presence of God.

![](/assets/uploads/sonnie-badu-glory-night-crowdsurf-bw.jpg)

Glory Night also featured several respected gospel ministers from different nations, highlighting the growing collaboration within the African Christian community. Their collective ministry underscored the power of worship to unite believers across cultures and denominations in pursuit of one purpose: to glorify God.

![](/assets/uploads/sonnie-badu-glory-night-solo-performance.jpg)

For Sonnie Badu, whose worship ministry has impacted millions around the world over the past two decades, the gathering was yet another testament to the enduring influence of gospel music in transforming lives and strengthening faith.

The overwhelming attendance at Stade de Kégué reflects the increasing popularity of large scale gospel worship events across West Africa, where Christians continue to embrace opportunities for corporate worship, prayer, and spiritual renewal.

![](/assets/uploads/sonnie-badu-glory-night-brass-band-crowd.jpg)

As worship echoed throughout the stadium, Glory Night stood as a powerful reminder of the unifying message of the Gospel and the continued growth of Christian worship movements across the region.
