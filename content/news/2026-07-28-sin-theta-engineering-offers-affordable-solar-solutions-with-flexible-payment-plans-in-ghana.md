**Accra, Ghana** – As power outages continue to affect parts of Accra, more households and businesses are turning to solar energy as a reliable alternative. According to officials of **Sin Theta Engineering Group**, access to quality solar power is no longer reserved for a few, thanks to flexible payment options and comprehensive installation services designed to make renewable energy more accessible.

Speaking on Sunny 88.7 FM's **Inspirational Morning Drive**, representatives of Sin Theta Engineering Group explained how the company's Commercial Division is helping individuals, schools, churches, healthcare facilities and businesses transition to solar energy without the burden of large upfront costs.

### One-Stop Shop for Solar and Electrical Solutions

Marketing Officer Eugenia Bako described the Commercial Division as the supply arm of Sin Theta Engineering Group, providing wholesale and retail electrical products, solar equipment, CCTV systems and complete electrical installation services.

She said the company is certified by the Energy Commission of Ghana and serves homeowners, institutions and commercial clients with quality products and expert technical advice.

![](/assets/uploads/sin-theta-eugenia-bako-interview.jpg)

"Whether you are building a new home, wiring a commercial facility or installing a solar system, we provide complete solutions from supply to installation," she explained.

### Flexible Payment Plans Remove the Cost Barrier

Recognising that cost remains one of the biggest concerns for prospective customers, Sin Theta Engineering has introduced several financing models to make solar systems more affordable.

For homeowners, the company's **Pay Small Small (PSS)** package allows customers to make a 50% down payment, with the remaining balance spread over up to 12 months.

For schools, the company offers a **Solar as a Service** programme that requires zero upfront payment. After assessing the institution's energy needs, Sin Theta installs the system and schools pay a fixed monthly fee. Similar solutions are available for healthcare facilities, while a business-focused package is currently being rolled out.

### Start Small and Upgrade Later

Project Manager Engineer Desmond Dake encouraged customers not to delay their transition to solar because of budget constraints.

He explained that clients can initially install only solar panels and an inverter, then add batteries later as finances permit.

"The inverter converts the direct current produced by solar panels into alternating current used by household appliances. Batteries can be added later to store excess energy," he explained.

![](/assets/uploads/sin-theta-desmond-dake-interview.jpg)

### Safety and Quality Remain a Priority

Engineer Dake stressed that safety is central to every Sin Theta installation.

According to him, all products used by the company are approved by the Ghana Standards Authority and the Energy Commission, while installations are carried out by certified professionals in accordance with recognised industry standards.

Every completed project undergoes inspection and testing before clients receive an electrical compliance certificate. The company also provides scheduled maintenance to ensure systems continue operating efficiently.

### Trusted by Leading Institutions

Sin Theta Engineering has completed projects for several educational institutions and major organisations across Ghana.

Among the institutions mentioned were Bright Lilies School, Teaching Aid School, USAID, ADB Bank, National Investment Bank (NIB) and Everpure, where the company undertook electrical installation works.

Officials noted that schools operating on solar have significantly reduced their dependence on the national electricity grid while maintaining uninterrupted teaching and learning.

### Solar Systems Protected by Insurance

One feature that distinguishes Sin Theta's residential solar package is insurance coverage.

Customers who sign up under the Pay Small Small programme receive insurance protection through Star Assurance, covering risks such as theft and certain natural disasters, providing additional peace of mind for their investment.
