*By Michael Ofosu-Afriyie, Kumasi*

Renowned American international evangelist Andrew Palau has strongly affirmed the unmatched power of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, describing it as the most potent, life-altering force capable of driving the holistic transformation that humanity earnestly seeks.

Addressing a press conference ahead of the highly anticipated Love Ghana Evangelistic Crusade at the iconic Kumasi Racecourse, Evangelist Palau emphasised that no secular philosophy or human intervention can match the profound potency embedded in the message of Jesus Christ.

He highlighted that the enduring narrative of Christ's miraculous birth, transformative earthly ministry, sacrificial death and triumphant resurrection serves as a divine testament, one that radicalises hearts, renews minds and reshapes destinies for all who believe.

> "I possess unshakeable faith in the power of the Gospel because it stands alone as the ultimate catalyst for complete human transformation. It possesses the unique ability to reshape individuals from the inside out, enabling them to reflect both the holy character of God and the self-giving love of Jesus Christ."

### Love Ghana Festival

Elaborating on the scope of the week-long spiritual awakening, Evangelist Palau outlined the primary objective of the Love Ghana Festival, which is to penetrate every corner of Kumasi with the redeeming and hopeful message of the Gospel.

He said the extensive outreach goes beyond the pulpit.

In a powerful demonstration of faith in action, the Luis Palau Association, in close partnership with local bodies, has been conducting free community health screenings across various schools and regional hospitals.

Evangelist Palau warmly commended the Kumasi Council of Churches for their unwavering support and collaborative spirit in facilitating this landmark initiative, the Palau team's first large-scale evangelistic drive in the historic Ashanti regional capital.

Highlighting the ministry's multi-faceted approach, Palau noted that his wife, Wendy Palau, alongside a dedicated international team, has been visiting dozens of educational institutions across the Ashanti Region.

Their mission continues to inspire students, teachers and administrative staff alike through encouraging words and practical aid.

### Legacy of faith

Evangelist Andrew Palau, son of the legendary late international evangelist Luis Palau, has spent over two decades leading massive evangelistic festivals around the globe.

Following in the footsteps of his father, Andrew's ministry is renowned for blending high-energy festival elements, such as action sports, live music and family fun, with a clear, uncompromised presentation of the Christian faith.

The Love Ghana Festival marks a significant milestone in bringing this global, holistic approach to soul-winning and humanitarian service directly to the heartland of Ghana.

### Call to residents

Echoing Palau's enthusiasm, Rev. Dr. Douglas Okonah Frimpong, a prominent member of the Love Ghana Festival Organising Committee, expressed profound gratitude to the Palau Ministry for their vision and commitment to the spiritual well-being of the city.

Rev. Dr. Frimpong commended the extensive logistical and material support provided by the Palau team, noting that it has immensely benefited local churches, volunteers and community members.

He urged families across Kumasi and surrounding areas to turn out in large numbers for the weekend festival, describing it as a dynamic, total-package event designed for all generations.

Before the main evening crusade services begin at 4:00 PM, attendees will enjoy:

- High-octane stunt motorcycling and BMX biking displays by world-class professional athletes
- Family-friendly recreational activities, including swimming and games
- Inspiring live musical performances by local and international gospel artists

"We are overjoyed to host Evangelist Andrew Palau and his dynamic team in Kumasi for this holistic outreach," Rev. Dr. Frimpong stated.

He added, "We are confident that Christ will be magnified at the Racecourse grounds this weekend as thousands gather to hear the Gospel, experience healing and make lifetime commitments to walk with God."
