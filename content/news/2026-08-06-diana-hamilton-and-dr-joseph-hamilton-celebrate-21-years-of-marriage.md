Gospel singer Diana Hamilton and her husband, Dr. Joseph Hamilton, are celebrating 21 years of marriage this week, marking more than two decades of partnership alongside her flourishing music ministry.

While Diana Hamilton has gone on to touch audiences worldwide with her worship music, those close to the couple describe Dr. Hamilton as a steady presence behind the scenes — supporting her calling with patience and encouragement through the demands of ministry life.

Their union is often pointed to as an example of what many describe as a God-centred home sustaining a public ministry: a marriage built on shared faith, sacrifice, and commitment, largely away from the spotlight that follows Diana Hamilton's career.

Well-wishers marking the anniversary describe the milestone as a reminder that the impact of a ministry isn't limited to the stage — it also rests on the faithfulness and unity maintained at home.

Congratulations pour in for Diana Hamilton and Dr. Joseph Hamilton on 21 years together, with well-wishers praying for continued strength in their marriage, their home, and the example they set for other couples pursuing shared purpose in faith.
