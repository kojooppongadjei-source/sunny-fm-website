The Chairman of The Church of Pentecost, Apostle Eric Nyamekye, has dedicated an ultramodern church facility for the Dr. Thomas Wyatt English Assembly and Children's Auditorium.

![Apostle Eric Nyamekye ministers at the dedication service, flanked by ministers and traditional leaders](/assets/uploads/cop-wyatt-assembly-dedication-wyatt_1.png)

Valued at over GH₵13 million, the project was largely funded by Elder Dr. Samuel Nana Amo Tobbin, CEO of Tobinco Pharmaceuticals and a long-standing member of the Wyatt Assembly. The new facility features a main auditorium, dedicated Children's and Youth Ministry centres, conference and meeting rooms, administrative offices, and ample parking space.

![The ribbon is cut at the dedication, with the Ga Mantse and other dignitaries in attendance](/assets/uploads/cop-wyatt-assembly-dedication-wyatt_2.png)

The ribbon was cut together with the Ga Mantse, underscoring the strong ties between the assembly and the traditional leadership of the Ga State, as church members, clergy, and community leaders gathered to witness the occasion.

![Inside the new auditorium](/assets/uploads/cop-wyatt-assembly-dedication-wyatt_3.png)

Dr. Thomas Wyatt Assembly, located in the Newtown District of the La Area in Accra, has grown steadily under The Church of Pentecost, and the new auditorium is expected to give the growing congregation — along with its children's and youth ministries — significantly more room to worship and serve.
