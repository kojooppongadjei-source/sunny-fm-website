### How to Be Street Smart

Bishop Kakra Baiden anchors this Airpower broadcast in Exodus 3:7-8 — God seeing His people's affliction and coming down to deliver them — to teach that spiritual maturity means being alert and discerning in the real, sometimes hostile world believers live in, not withdrawn from it. "Street smart," in his framing, is the ability to recognise the tactics of the enemy early and respond with wisdom rather than naivety or fear.

He teaches that many believers suffer avoidable setbacks not because God is absent, but because they failed to read the warning signs God had already placed in front of them — through the Word, through the Spirit's promptings, or through wise counsel. Bishop Baiden encourages listeners to sharpen their spiritual senses through consistent time in prayer and Scripture, so that discernment becomes instinctive rather than something scrambled for in a crisis.

The message closes with a charge to combine boldness with wisdom — living unafraid in the world while refusing to be caught off guard by it, trusting that the same God who saw Israel's affliction is actively watching over His people today.
