### Walking in the Light

In this broadcast, Bishop David Oyedepo unpacks what it means to live as a child of light in a world clouded by confusion and compromise. Drawing from 1 John 1:7 — "if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship one with another" — he teaches that light is not simply knowledge, but obedience: a believer who walks in the light lives transparently before God and others, with nothing hidden and nothing to fear.

Bishop Oyedepo challenges listeners to examine where compromise has crept into their walk with God, insisting that half-hearted faith cannot carry the weight of a believer's calling. He ties this directly to faith and action, urging believers not to merely hear the Word but to build their lives on it, since it is obedience — not mere information — that positions a believer for divine light to guide every decision.

The message closes with a call to consecration: to deliberately choose righteousness even when it costs something, because walking in the light is what keeps a believer's fellowship with God — and with fellow believers — unbroken.
