### The Scent of Favour

Rev. Eastwood Anaba opens this teaching with the picture of Isaac blessing Jacob in Genesis 27 — "the smell of my son is as the smell of a field which the Lord hath blessed" — and builds on it to explain what he calls "the scent of favour": an unmistakable, God-given distinction that draws opportunity, honour, and blessing to a person's life without them having to force it.

He teaches that favour is not earned by striving but released through identity — that once a believer understands who they are in Christ, doors open that human effort alone could never open. Rev. Anaba draws on his own journey, from a pharmacy career into full-time ministry, as an illustration that God's favour often calls people away from the safe and familiar into the assignment He has actually prepared for them.

The broadcast closes with practical steps for "carrying the scent" daily — a life of worship, generosity, and obedience — so that favour is not a one-time encounter but a continual atmosphere a believer walks in.
