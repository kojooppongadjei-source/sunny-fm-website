### Become a Man and Woman of Prayer

Pastor Andy Yawson teaches that prayer is not a religious formality but the believer's direct line of authority — the place where heaven's resources are released into everyday, practical living. He walks through the model Jesus gave His disciples in Luke 11, showing that consistency in prayer, more than eloquence, is what marks someone as a true "man or woman of prayer."

He challenges the common excuse of busyness, pointing out that every believer who has walked closely with God — from Daniel to the early church in Acts — treated prayer as non-negotiable rather than optional. Pastor Yawson also addresses distraction, urging listeners to build a fixed time and place for prayer so it becomes a discipline rather than a reaction to crisis.

The broadcast closes with an encouragement that a praying life, sustained daily rather than occasionally, is what positions a believer to "win" in every area — family, finances, health, and purpose — echoing the Winning Ways Express series' focus on practical Christian living.
