Accra, the countdown is on! In just a few days, the **UPSA Auditorium** will host **Royal Praise 2026** — an unforgettable gathering of worship, praise, and life-changing encounters with God.

Join **Obaapa Adwoa Christy** and other anointed ministers, including **Empress Gifty**, **Joyce Blessing**, and **Uncle Ato**, for an evening you won't want to miss.

### Event Details

* **Date:** Sunday, 9th August 2026
* **Venue:** UPSA Auditorium, Accra
* **Time:** 4:00 PM

### Who's Ministering

* **Obaapa Adwoa Christy**
* **Empress Gifty**
* **Joyce Blessing**
* **Uncle Ato**

### Why You Shouldn't Miss It

Royal Praise 2026 is more than a concert — it's a gathering built for encounter. Some of Ghana's most anointed gospel voices will lead the city in a night of praise, worship, and ministry designed to lift spirits, strengthen faith, and draw hearts closer to God.

Whether you're coming to worship, to be refreshed, or simply to be in an atmosphere of unrestrained praise, Royal Praise 2026 is set to be a moment Accra won't forget.

**Come with expectation. Invite your family and friends.**

Don't miss it — Sunday, 9th August 2026, 4PM, at the UPSA Auditorium.
