Ready for a weekend that's equal parts adventure and connection? **Sunny Singles Adventure — Volta Edition** takes singles to some of Ghana's most beautiful natural sights for a getaway built around fun, fitness, and meeting new people.

### What's Included

* **Conquer Afadjato** — Ghana's highest peak
* **Experience Tagbo Falls**
* **Buffet dinner and buffet breakfast**
* **Transportation**
* **Lunch & snack**
* **Mingle hangout**
* **Singles Lounge Conversation**

### Facilitator — Singles Lounge Conversation

**Kobina Jackson**, Founder of Doubles, facilitates the Singles Lounge Conversation.

### Event Details

* **Dates:** 31st October – 1st November 2026
* **Single occupancy:** GHC 2,850
* **Double occupancy:** GHC 4,770
* **Registration deadline:** 26th October 2026
