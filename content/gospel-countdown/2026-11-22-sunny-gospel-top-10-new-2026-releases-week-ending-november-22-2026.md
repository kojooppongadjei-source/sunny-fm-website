## This week's highlights

**Piesie Esther takes our first #1** with "Great Change (Nsesaeɛ Kɛseɛ)." Released this year, the song has passed five million views on YouTube in about two months, making it the biggest new gospel song in Ghana right now. She has three songs in the top 10, more than any other artist: her live performance of "Atɛɛne (Overturned)," recorded at her Flora Made by Grace concert, is at #5, and "Calvary Sunsum" with Kweku Teye, one of the most-played gospel songs on Ghanaian radio, is at #10.

**Diana Hamilton is at #2** with "Nyame Banbofo (God the Protector)." It was released at the end of July as the lead single from her forthcoming album, and it's a worship anthem declaring God's unfailing protection and faithfulness, sung in Twi and English.

**Adom Kiki is at #3** with "Nyame Aboa Me," a powerful song of thanksgiving that has passed a million views on YouTube.

**Vessel Chordrick's "Menye Wo Den Nie (Live)" is at #4.** It's the fastest-growing song on the streaming platforms this week, with streams up more than 18% in seven days. His "The Hope Medley" is also on the chart at #9.

**Daughters of Glorious Jesus have two songs on the chart.** They bring back their 1992 classic "Bue M'ani" in a new recording with Sandra Boakye-Duah at #6, and their Afro-flavoured "Yɛdi Adeɛ," inspired by Romans 8:17, is at #7. **Joe Mettle's hopeful anthem "This Year" is at #8.**

## How the chart works

The Sunny Gospel Top 10 ranks gospel songs by Ghanaian artists that were **released in 2026**, including new recordings of older songs, based on how many plays they got on streaming platforms and YouTube during the week.

We also screen the chart to make sure every song comes from real artists and ministries. We remove AI-generated and automated tracks, and older recordings that were only re-uploaded to streaming platforms in 2026.
